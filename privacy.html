算算派对 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月（随 2.0 版本生效）· 提供方：YushenZhang

一句话

算算派对不收集你的任何个人信息：没有账号，没有服务器，没有广告，没有统计，也不接入任何第三方 SDK。

我们不收集什么

不收集姓名、邮箱、电话、联系人、位置、照片、麦克风或摄像头内容、广告标识符、设备标识符、使用统计和崩溃报告。App 不向我们的任何服务器发送数据（我们没有服务器）。

保存在你设备上的内容

答题进度、设置、奖杯、收藏和每日任务只保存在你的设备本地，用来恢复进度和离线游戏。为了在 App 被系统清理后恢复进度，App 会在设备本地保留一份存档备份；这份备份可能被包含在你自己的 iPhone / iPad 备份（例如 iCloud 备份）里，由苹果按你的系统设置处理，我们无法访问。删除 App 会删除这些存档。

应用内购买

“解锁全部”是一次性买断，由 Apple 的 App Store 处理。付款信息和购买记录由 Apple 管理，我们不会收到你的姓名、账户或支付信息；App 只会向系统查询“这个商品是否已购买”。家人共享、恢复购买和退款均按 Apple 的规则执行。

每日提醒（可选）

只有在你于设置中打开“每日提醒”时，App 才会向系统申请通知权限，并在设备本地排定提醒通知。提醒完全在设备上生成，不经过任何服务器；你可以随时在设置或系统“通知”里关闭。

震动与声音

App 使用设备的触感反馈和音频播放游戏音乐与音效；不录音，不使用麦克风。

网络

游戏主体、音乐和全部文字都随 App 离线提供，游戏过程不需要网络。只有读取内购价格、购买或恢复购买时，系统才会与 Apple 的 App Store 通信（由 Apple 处理，不经过我们）。

儿童

算算派对面向成人和青少年，不属于儿童类别。App 没有账号、社交、广告或数据收集功能，因此不会收集任何人（包括儿童）的个人信息。

政策变更

如果以后功能变化影响数据处理，我们会先更新本页，并在 App 更新说明中提示。

SumSum Party Privacy Policy

Effective October 2026 (version 2.0) · Provided by YushenZhang

In one sentence

SumSum Party collects none of your personal information: no account, no server, no ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera content, advertising or device identifiers, usage statistics, or crash reports. The app sends no data to any server of ours (we do not run one).

What stays on your device

Your progress, settings, trophies, collection and daily missions are stored locally on your device so the game works offline and can resume. To restore progress if the system clears the app's data, the app keeps a backup copy of the save on the device; that copy may be included in your own iPhone / iPad backup (for example iCloud Backup), which Apple handles according to your system settings and which we cannot access. Deleting the app deletes the save.

In-app purchase

"Unlock Everything" is a one-time purchase handled by Apple's App Store. Apple manages payment details and purchase records; we never receive your name, account or payment information. The app only asks the system whether the product has been purchased. Family Sharing, restoring purchases and refunds follow Apple's rules.

Daily reminder (optional)

Only when you turn on "Daily reminder" in Settings does the app ask the system for notification permission and schedule local notifications. They are generated on your device and never pass through a server. You can turn them off in Settings or in iOS Notifications at any time.

Haptics and sound

The app uses your device's haptics and audio to play the game's music and sound effects. It does not record audio and does not use the microphone.

Network

The game, its music and all text ship inside the app and work offline. The system talks to Apple's App Store only to read the price, to buy, or to restore a purchase (handled by Apple, never through us).

Children

SumSum Party is made for adults and teenagers and is not in the Kids category. It has no accounts, social features, ads or data collection, so it collects no one's personal information, children included.

Changes

If a future feature changes how data is handled, we will update this page first and mention it in the update notes.
