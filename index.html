常见问题 / FAQ

怎么恢复购买？ 设置 ▸ 解锁 ▸ 恢复购买。需要使用购买时的 Apple ID。
How do I restore my purchase? Settings ▸ Unlock ▸ Restore Purchases, with the Apple ID you bought with.

换手机后进度还在吗？ 进度保存在设备本地；通过 iCloud 或电脑备份恢复设备时会一起恢复。
Is my progress kept on a new phone? Progress is stored on the device and comes back when you restore it from an iCloud or computer backup.

怎么关闭每日提醒？ 设置 ▸ 提醒，或 iOS 设置 ▸ 通知。
How do I turn off the daily reminder? Settings ▸ Reminders, or iOS Settings ▸ Notifications.
